In 1963, a man in Derinkuyu, central Türkiye, knocked down a wall in his basement and found a passage going down. Behind it was an entire underground city: at least eight levels, about 85 meters deep, with room for some 20,000 people and their animals.

In this documentary we go down level by level: stables, kitchens black with soot, wine presses, wells, a chapel, more than fifty air shafts, and the huge rolling stone doors that sealed each floor from the inside. Who started digging it? Why did people hide here for centuries? And why are parts of it still unexplored?
