Frequently asked

Is VibeUpMe free?

Downloading the app and chatting after a match is free. Extras like super likes and boosts use coins, and none of them are required.

How is the frequency calculated?

We compare shared interests, zodiac compatibility, music taste, your vibe test result and what each of you is looking for. No AI is involved, the rules are open, and tapping a card shows why.

Who can use it?

VibeUpMe is for people aged 18 and over.

Is my data safe?

Your data is processed under KVKK, photos are served with signed, expiring links, and you can delete your account any time.

When is it launching?

Android and iOS versions are coming very soon. We'll announce it on this page.
