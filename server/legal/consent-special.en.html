To match you with others we need to know who you want to see (the gender you're interested in). Together with your gender this may reveal your sexual orientation, which is special category personal data and can only be processed with your explicit consent.

What and why

Your gender and the gender you're interested in are used only to show you suitable people and to show you to them. They are never used for advertising or profiling, never shared with third parties and never transferred abroad.

If you don't consent

Matching can't work without it, so discover and likes are unavailable and your profile isn't shown in discover. Your existing chats continue.

Withdrawing

You can withdraw at any time under Profile › Privacy & my data; your profile is then removed from discover.
