The blue check shows that the person in the profile is really you. For that we ask for a selfie in a specific pose.

How it's used

Only our authorised verification team reviews your selfie by hand, comparing it with your profile photos. No automated face recognition is used; it isn't shown to other users or transferred abroad. Every view is logged.

How long it's kept

As proof of verification while your account is open. When you withdraw consent your selfies are deleted immediately (your blue check stays; a pending application is cancelled).

Withdrawing

Any time under Profile › Privacy & my data.
