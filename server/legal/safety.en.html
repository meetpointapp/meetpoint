A few tips to meet people safely on MeetPoint, and where to get help.

Safety tips

  • Never send money. Anyone asking for money, gift cards or crypto, for any reason, is very likely a scammer. Report them.
  • Chat in the app first. Be careful with people who want to move to another app right away; you lose our protection and reporting tools.
  • Protect your personal details: home address, workplace, bank details and ID.
  • Meet in a public place, arrange your own transport and tell a friend where you'll be.
  • On video calls, use the flag to report and end if someone asks for nudity or recordings. If you're threatened with images (sextortion), don't pay, keep the evidence and contact the police.

If something goes wrong

  • Use Report and Block on the profile or chat. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; reports about minors and explicit content come first.
  • Emergency in Türkiye: 112. Police: 155. Social support: 183.
