4. Delivery

Coins are credited to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed (instant performance). If crediting is delayed, open a request under Profile › Help & support.

5. Right of withdrawal

Coins are intangible goods delivered electronically and instantly. Under Art. 15/1-ğ of the Distance Contracts Regulation, since you consent to immediate delivery, the right of withdrawal does not apply. You give this consent separately in the App before your first purchase. Your statutory rights for defective performance (e.g. coins not credited) remain.

6. Refunds

Refund requests made through the stores follow Apple's and Google's refund policies. Coins of a refunded purchase are deducted from your account; if already spent, your balance may go negative.

7. Complaints

For complaints use in-app support or {{email}}. Disputes may be brought before consumer arbitration committees within the monetary limits announced yearly by the Ministry of Trade, and consumer courts above those limits.
