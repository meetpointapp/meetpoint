1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself), language preference.
  • Profile data: name, date of birth, gender, the gender you are interested in, photos, bio, interests, prompts, city and optional basics (height, job, education, zodiac, smoking/drinking).
  • Verification selfie: if you apply for the blue check, the selfie you take. Only our verification team sees it; it is never shown to other users.
  • Interactions: likes, matches, messages, contact requests, blocks and reports.
  • Transactions: coin purchases, spending, earnings and refunds.
  • Calls: who you called and when, call length and cost, gifts sent and the rating you give afterwards. Call audio and video are never recorded.
  • Cash-outs: if you cash out your earnings, your IBAN and account holder name or your PayPal email.
  • Approximate location: your location rounded to about 1 km; other users only see an approximate distance.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and app version, error logs.

Information such as your gender preference may be considered sensitive data; we process it only with your explicit consent and only to provide the matching service.

2. Why we process it

  • To create your account, show your profile and match you with suitable people (performance of contract),
  • To run messaging, contact requests and coin transactions (performance of contract),
  • To prevent fake accounts, fraud and abuse and to review reports (legitimate interest),
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g. accounting records, lawful requests from authorities).

3. Who we share it with

Your profile is shown to other users; your email address and verification selfie are not. We do not sell your data. We share data only as needed with service providers such as hosting, email delivery and payments (Apple, Google), our real-time communication provider for voice/video calls (Agora), and banks or PayPal for cash-out payments. Some providers may be located abroad; transfers are made with appropriate safeguards.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, messages, call history and verification selfie are deleted. Transaction records we are legally required to keep (including paid cash-outs) are retained for the period required by law. You can't delete your account while a cash-out request is pending; cancel it or wait for it to be processed first.
