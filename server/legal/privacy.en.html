1. What we process

  • Account: email address, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself), language, versions of the texts you accepted.
  • Profile: name, date of birth, gender, photos, bio, interests, prompts, city and optional basics.
  • Sexual orientation (special category): who you want to see. Processed only with your explicit consent, for matching.
  • Verification selfie: if you apply for the blue check, with your explicit consent.
  • Interactions: likes, matches, messages, requests, blocks and reports.
  • Calls: with whom, when, how long, cost, gifts and your rating. Audio and video are never recorded.
  • Finance: coin purchases, spending, earnings, refunds; for payouts your IBAN and account holder name or PayPal address (stored encrypted).
  • Approximate location: rounded to ~1 km; others only see an approximate distance.
  • Security and technical: sessions (device name, IP), sign-in attempts, error logs.

2. Why and on what legal basis

  • Creating your account, showing your profile, messaging, calls and coin transactions: performance of a contract,
  • Accounting, tax and requests from authorities: legal obligation,
  • Preventing fake accounts, fraud and abuse, account security, handling reports: legitimate interest,
  • Sexual orientation, verification selfie, transfers abroad and marketing: explicit consent.

3. Who receives it

Your profile is shown to other users; your email and selfie are not. We never sell your data. We share it as needed with hosting and email providers, Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat (purchases, abroad), Agora (calls, abroad) and Firebase (notifications, abroad) — the last two only with your separate consent; without it you can use everything except calls and push notifications — banks or PayPal (payouts), and authorities when legally required. Transfers abroad rely on standard contractual safeguards (KVKK art. 9) and, additionally, your consent.

4. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. Details: retention and deletion policy. In short: when you delete your account it is hidden at once and permanently deleted after 30 days unless you sign in again; accounts with no sign-in for 2 years are deleted after a 30-day email notice; paid payout and sales records are kept for the legally required period. Expired data is deleted automatically and every deletion is logged.

5. Your rights

You can learn whether and how your data is processed, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, object, and withdraw consent. In the app under Profile › Privacy & my data you can manage consents, download your data (once a month), send a request (answered within 30 days) and delete your account. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted; passwords are hashed with Argon2id and bank details are encrypted at field level; staff access is limited, protected by two-factor authentication and every action is logged immutably. In case of a data breach we notify the Authority and affected users as soon as possible.
