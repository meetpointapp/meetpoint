We ask for your permission to send you offers and news about new features. Permission is per channel; you can give or withdraw each one separately:

  • Email: offer and news emails (registered with Turkey's Message Management System, İYS).
  • Notifications: offers and news delivered as app notifications.

Required account messages (codes, security alerts, payment and support replies, deletion notices) don't depend on these permissions.

You can withdraw at any time, free of charge, under Profile › Notifications, via the link in any such email, or through İYS. Opt-outs are processed within 3 business days.
