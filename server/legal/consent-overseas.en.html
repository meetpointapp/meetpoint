Voice and video calls and push notifications run through providers whose servers are outside Türkiye. We ask for your explicit consent to this transfer.

Which services, which data

  • Agora (calls): the live audio/video stream and a call identifier. Nothing is recorded.
  • Google Firebase (notifications): your device's push token and the notification text.

If you don't consent

You can't make or receive calls and won't get push notifications. Everything else works as usual.

Withdrawing

You can give or withdraw consent at any time under Profile › Privacy & my data.
