To keep MeetPoint safe and respectful we expect everyone to follow these rules. Breaking them leads to escalating actions: warning → 24-hour restriction → 7-day restriction → permanent ban. Severe violations (★) can lead to an immediate permanent ban. You can appeal each decision once in the app.

Not allowed

  • ★ Users under 18; any content or contact involving minors.
  • ★ Nudity and sexual content (profile photos, chat photos and video calls); offering sexual services.
  • ★ Scams: asking for money, gift cards, crypto or investments; impersonation.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, persistent unwanted contact.
  • Fake profiles or other people's photos.
  • Spam and accounts that exist to push people to other platforms.

How we moderate

Reports are reviewed by our team by priority. Some automatic checks run: suspicious new profile photos are hidden until reviewed; sharing phone numbers, bank details or social handles in chat shows a safety tip; bulk messaging and bursts of reports are queued for review, and an account reported by several people in a short time may be restricted for 24 hours while we review. Automatic checks never make permanent decisions on their own.

What a restriction means

You can still sign in and read your chats, but you can't send messages, photos, likes or requests, make calls or edit your profile.
