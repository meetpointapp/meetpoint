To help us improve the app, we'd like your permission to track your usage milestones (registration completed, matched, first message sent, first coin purchase) as anonymous counts. You can turn this on if you want, and turn it off again at any time.

  • Never shared with a third party: the data stays only on our own server; we do not use an external analytics service such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel.
  • Milestones only: at most one record is kept per milestone (registration, match, first message, first purchase); we do not track detailed behavior such as which profiles you view, what you write, or when you're online.
  • Includes your state before consenting: when you turn this on, any milestones you had already reached (e.g. if you'd already matched with someone) are recorded once as of that moment — this reflects data the app already keeps for its own operation, not new tracking.
  • Only totals appear in the admin panel: the admin panel shows aggregate counts like "how many people registered, matched, sent a message, purchased" — never an individual user's behavior.

You can withdraw this permission for free at any time from Profile › Privacy and my data; once withdrawn, no new records are kept, and if you delete your account, existing records are deleted along with it.
